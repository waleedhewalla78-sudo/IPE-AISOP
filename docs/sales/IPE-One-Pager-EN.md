# IPE — AI Planning for Your Factory

### *Know which orders are at risk before the shift starts*

---

## The Problem

Your planner maintains Excel alongside Odoo — two systems, double the work, and neither tells you what's about to go wrong. Infeasibility is discovered on the shop floor, not in the morning planning meeting, when it's too late to act without chaos and cost. Management sees on-time delivery problems at month-end, in a report, long after the damage is done.

---

## The Solution

IPE connects directly to your Odoo ERP and scores every Manufacturing Order for feasibility across five constraint gates — material availability, capacity, bill of materials, demand, and delivery date. At-risk orders surface automatically in a risk queue, sorted by financial impact, with 2–3 structured resolution scenarios showing the cost and delivery-date trade-off for each option. An Arabic dashboard gives your leadership real-time on-time delivery visibility — not a monthly report, but a live view updated every 15 minutes.
